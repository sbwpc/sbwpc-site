# SBWPC membership system: decisions and status

Updated: November 13, 2026

## Decisions
- Membership term: 12 months from the payment date. A member who renews before the expiration date keeps the remaining time (the new term starts from the old expiration date). A lapsed member starts fresh from the payment date.
- Admins: info@sbwpc.org and luke@coolhandlook.com, both full admins. A treasurer (read and export only) role exists for later.
- Email: receipts and reminders will send from the Google Workspace mailbox info@sbwpc.org. Newsletters stay in Constant Contact.
- Reminders: 30 days before, 7 days before, on the day, and one lapsed notice 14 days after.
- Households: one primary member record, with the second person's name stored on the membership.
- Code repository: public. It was made private on November 12, 2026, but Netlify's current plan will not build a private repository from outside contributors, so it was switched back the same day. No member data or secret keys are in the code.
- Older member list (before May 2026): unknown. Ask the client.

## If an admin loses their phone
A Supabase organization member opens Authentication, Users, picks the person, and removes their two-factor factor. The person then signs in with their password and sets up two-factor again.

## To add or remove an admin
1. Add or deactivate the email in the admins table (Supabase, Table Editor).
2. Invite the person under Authentication, Users, Invite user.

## Stage 2 (admin back end, on-site forms, payments): deployed November 13, 2026
The admin back end is live at /admin. The new forms are live only on the two test pages and cannot take payments until the Netlify settings below are entered. The live /join and /donate pages still use JotForm.

### What was built
- Admin back end at /admin: overview, members list, donors and payments list, checks to confirm, one page per person, add and edit people, memberships and notes, record a check or cash payment by hand, CSV downloads (members, payments with FPPC fields, email list of people who gave permission). Every change is written to the audit log by the database.
- New membership and donation forms, on two test pages that are not linked and are hidden from search engines: /join-preview and /donate-preview. The live /join and /donate pages still use JotForm.
- Three ways to pay: PayPal (including card through PayPal), Apple Pay (one-time payments), or a mailed check. A check is saved as a promise and only counts as paid when an admin marks it received.
- Yearly auto-renew for memberships and monthly or yearly donations, as PayPal subscriptions.
- Server functions (web/netlify/functions): checkout-config, checkout-create, checkout-capture, paypal-webhook, backup.
- Database: supabase/migrations/002_checkout.sql (checkouts and settings tables, record_payment and confirm_check). Applied to the live project on November 13, 2026. Member counts were unchanged (61 people, 58 memberships, 61 payments).
- Nightly encrypted backup: every table, AES-256-GCM, kept 30 nights in Netlify Blobs (store "member-backups"). Opens only with the backup key. To read one: supabase/backup/restore.mjs.

### Rules the system follows
- The price always comes from the server's own list (web/src/lib/giving.js), never from the browser.
- Nothing is marked paid until PayPal confirms the payment and the amount matches.
- The same PayPal payment can never be recorded twice.
- A renewal with the same email updates the existing person and membership. No duplicate.
- Online checkouts that were never finished are deleted after 30 days.
