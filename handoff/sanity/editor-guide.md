# How to update sbwpc.org

## Sign in
Go to https://sbwpc.sanity.studio/ and sign in with info@sbwpc.org. Click "Start here" in the left menu for shortcuts.

## How it works
1. Pick what you want to change in the left menu.
2. Click the item, then change the words or photos.
3. Click the green Publish button at the bottom right.
4. Wait about one minute. The change then shows on sbwpc.org.

Nothing goes on the website until you click Publish. Your work is saved as you type, so you can stop and come back.

## Common jobs
**Post news.** Click News, then the + button at the top. Fill in the title, date, a short summary and a photo. Click Publish.

**Add an event.** Click Events, then +. Fill in the title, start time and place. Use Buttons for a sign-up link. Click Publish. Events move to the Past list on their own.

**Add an endorsement.** Click Endorsements, then +. Choose the election, then the office and the candidate. Click Publish.

**Change a board member.** Click Board of Directors, then the person. Change the name, role or photo. Click Publish.

**Edit the words on a page.** Click Pages, pick the section, then the page. Click the block you want to change. Click Publish. To add a block, click Add item and pick one from the picture menu. Drag blocks to reorder them.

**Change the menu, footer or top banner.** Click Site settings. Use the tabs at the top.

## Photos
- Drag a photo onto the photo box or click Upload.
- Fill in "Photo description" with one short sentence, like "Board members at the 2026 candidate forum". It helps people who cannot see the photo.
- Use a photo at least 1600 pixels wide. A JPG under 2 MB works well.

## Good to know
- A yellow warning is advice. You can still publish. A red message means something is missing.
- Pages and Site settings cannot be deleted. This protects the website.
- A page address locks a few minutes after the page is created. Ask for help if one must change.
- If you are not sure, do not delete anything. Ask first.
