# SBWPC membership system: decisions and status

Updated: November 12, 2026

## Decisions
- Membership term: 12 months from the payment date. A member who renews before the expiration date keeps the remaining time (the new term starts from the old expiration date). A lapsed member starts fresh from the payment date.
- Admins: info@sbwpc.org and luke@coolhandlook.com, both full admins. A treasurer (read and export only) role exists for later.
- Email: receipts and reminders will send from the Google Workspace mailbox info@sbwpc.org. Newsletters stay in Constant Contact.
- Reminders: 30 days before, 7 days before, on the day, and one lapsed notice 14 days after.
- Households: one primary member record, with the second person's name stored on the membership.
- Code repository: private (changed November 12, 2026).
- Older member list (before May 2026): unknown. Ask the client.

## Stage 1 (foundation): built, waiting for deploy
- Database tables, access rules and audit log: supabase/migrations/001_foundation.sql (applied to the live project).
- Access tests: supabase/tests/access_rules.sql (run against a local copy; all pass). Checked again from outside against the live project: every table refuses the public key.
- Public sign-ups are turned off. Accounts are created by invitation only.
- Admin page: /admin (sign in, required two-factor, summary dashboard). Not linked from the site, hidden from search engines, never cached, session ends when the tab closes or after 30 minutes idle.
- Not done yet: nightly encrypted backup. It needs a server-side key in Netlify, which comes with stage 2.

## If an admin loses their phone
A Supabase organization member opens Authentication, Users, picks the person, and removes their two-factor factor. The person then signs in with their password and sets up two-factor again.

## To add or remove an admin
1. Add or deactivate the email in the admins table (Supabase, Table Editor).
2. Invite the person under Authentication, Users, Invite user.
