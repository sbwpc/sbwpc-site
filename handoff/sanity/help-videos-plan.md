# Help videos for the SBWPC Studio

Goal: the client can answer her own questions, so training and support time stays small.

## Rules
- One video per job. 60 to 90 seconds each.
- Title each video as the question she would ask, such as "How do I add an event?"
- Use real SBWPC content in every recording.
- Captions on every video. Voice is optional.
- End each video with what happens next ("The website updates in about a minute").

## Where she finds them
- A "Help videos" item in the left menu, listed by question.
- A "Watch" link on each Start here card.
- A link in the help text of the field people get stuck on.

## Videos
1. Sign in and find your way around
2. Post a news item
3. Add an event (and how upcoming and past events sort)
4. Add or change an endorsement
5. Change a board member or a photo
6. Edit the words on a page
7. Add, move or remove a block on a page
8. Add a photo and write its description
9. Publish, and what to do if the site has not changed
10. Undo a mistake (version history)
11. Change the menu, footer or top banner
12. Members: record a check, download a list
13. Lost your phone, and who to ask for help

## Open choices
- Where videos are hosted: unlisted YouTube or Vimeo (easy, adds an outside account), or files on sbwpc.org (no outside account, no ads, we control them).
- Who records: Luke records with his own voice from a script and shot list, or silent captioned screen recordings made for him.
