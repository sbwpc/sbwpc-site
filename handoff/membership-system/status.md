Updated: November 14, 2026

## Stage 2 (admin back end, on-site forms, payments): deployed November 13, 2026
The admin back end is live at /admin. The new forms are live only on the two test pages and cannot take payments until the Netlify settings below are entered. The live /join and /donate pages still use JotForm.

### What was built
- Admin back end at /admin: overview, members list, donors and payments list, checks to confirm, one page per person, add and edit people, memberships and notes, record a check or cash payment by hand, CSV downloads (members, payments with FPPC fields, email list of people who gave permission). Every change is written to the audit log by the database.
- New membership and donation forms, on two test pages that are not linked and are hidden from search engines: /join-preview and /donate-preview. The live /join and /donate pages still use JotForm.
- Three ways to pay: PayPal (including card through PayPal), Apple Pay (one-time payments), or a mailed check. A check is saved as a promise and only counts as paid when an admin marks it received.
- Yearly auto-renew for memberships and monthly or yearly donations, as PayPal subscriptions.
- Server functions (web/netlify/functions): checkout-config, checkout-create, checkout-capture, paypal-webhook, backup.
- Database: supabase/migrations/002_checkout.sql (checkouts and settings tables, record_payment and confirm_check). Applied to the live project on November 13, 2026. Member counts were unchanged (61 people, 58 memberships, 61 payments).
- Nightly encrypted backup: every table, AES-256-GCM, kept 30 nights in Netlify Blobs (store "member-backups"). Opens only with the backup key. To read one: supabase/backup/restore.mjs.

### Rules the system follows
- The price always comes from the server's own list (web/src/lib/giving.js), never from the browser.
- Nothing is marked paid until PayPal confirms the payment and the amount matches.
- The same PayPal payment can never be recorded twice.
- A renewal with the same email updates the existing person and membership. No duplicate.
- Online checkouts that were never finished are deleted after 30 days.

## Stage 3 (email): built and tested locally, not deployed
Branch: mailer (built on sandbox-ready). Decided November 14, 2026: send through a Google Cloud service account as info@sbwpc.org; Claude drafts the wording and the client approves it.

### What was built
- Receipts to members and donors after every online payment, and when an admin marks a check received or records a payment by hand (with a tick box to skip it).
- "How to mail your check" email to anyone who chooses Mail a check.
- Renewal reminders 30 days before, 7 days before, on the day, and once 14 days after a membership expires. Each has a private renew link that opens the Join form with the level selected. The link changes after every renewal. Members on automatic renewal get one heads-up about the coming charge instead.
- Notices to info@sbwpc.org for each new member, renewal, donation, promised check, and failed or cancelled automatic payment.
- Admin sign-in emails (invitation, password reset) sent from info@sbwpc.org through a Supabase Send Email hook.
- Admin: an Emails list on each person page, and an "Email a renewal reminder" button.
- Database: supabase/migrations/003_mail.sql (outbox table, updated record_payment, renewal_prefill). NOT yet applied to the live project.
- Server: web/netlify/lib/mail.mjs, emails.mjs, outbox.mjs and the functions mail-queue (every 5 minutes), reminders (daily, 8:30 AM Pacific in winter), auth-email, checkout-renew.

### How it behaves
- Every email is a row in the outbox table: queued, sent, failed or not sent, with the reason. That is the record.
- A mail problem never blocks a payment. Failed sends are retried three times.
- MAIL_MODE controls sending. Unset or off: nothing is sent and nothing piles up. test: everything goes to MAIL_TEST_TO, labelled with the real recipient. live: normal.
- No reminder is due for the imported members until April 2027. Their memberships expire from May 14, 2027.

## Stage 4 (members cancel their own automatic payments): built and tested locally, not deployed
Branch: manage-plan (built on mailer). Built November 14, 2026.

### What was built
- A page at /manage where a member sees their automatic payments (yearly membership renewal, monthly or yearly gift) and can stop one. Not in the menu, hidden from search engines, left out of the sitemap.
- The page opens from a private link. The link is in every receipt for an automatic payment and in the heads-up email before a yearly charge. A member without the link types their email on /manage and the link is sent to that address.
- Stopping asks "are you sure" first. PayPal is told to stop charging. Only when PayPal agrees is the plan marked cancelled. A membership already paid for stays active until it expires, with automatic renewal off, and the normal renewal reminders then apply.
- Emails: a confirmation to the member, and a notice to info@sbwpc.org that says whether it was cancelled on the website or in PayPal.
- Admin: the Recurring plans panel on a person page shows the cancelled date and has an "Email them the manage link" button.
- Database: supabase/migrations/004_manage_plan.sql (two new kinds of email, and the private link is hidden from everyone signed in to the admin). NOT yet applied to the live project.
- Server: web/netlify/lib/plans.mjs and the functions plan-view, plan-cancel, plan-link. The PayPal webhook now uses the same code, so a repeated message from PayPal never sends a second notice.

### Rules it follows
- The page shows a first name and the plans. No email, address or PayPal number.
- One person's link can never open or cancel another person's plan.
- The "email me my link" form answers the same way whether or not the address has a plan, and sends at most one email per person every 15 minutes.
- If PayPal cannot be reached, nothing is marked cancelled and the page says so.
- A plan with no PayPal record is never marked cancelled by the page. The member is asked to email SBWPC.
- The private link is kept out of the audit log, out of the admin, and out of web addresses sent to the server.
